# MING private-beta test script

Updated: 2026-10-18. This is a usability and calibration exercise, not a scientific accuracy study. Use with willing adults only. Do not ask a tester to disclose sensitive life events or send their birth details to the project team.

## Invite and consent

Suggested invitation: “I am testing a Chinese BaZi reading website. It is experimental and may be wrong. Would you spend about 15 minutes trying it, then tell me where it felt useful, vague, or uncomfortable? You can skip any question and stop at any time. The website sends birth details to an AI provider to generate the reading.”

The tester uses the website directly. Ask for broad age band, country/cultural setting, whether birth time is known, and whether they already know BaZi or astrology. Do not collect their exact birth date, name, city, report, or private timeline unless they separately agree to share it. Never ask for medical, trauma, financial, or third-party details.

## Before opening the result

If the tester is comfortable, have them privately note 2–4 concrete changes from their past, with approximate years. This note belongs to the tester; they need not show it. It reduces the temptation to reinterpret vague website text as a “hit” after the fact. Skipping this step is fine, but mark the session as not pre-recorded.

## During the reading

Observe without coaching. Record whether they understand Date → Time → City, whether they can proceed with unknown birth time, time to reach Looking Back, whether they notice the bottom Next control, and whether Chinese/English mode stays consistent. Ask them to read each highlighted “checkable event” before opening its rationale or alternatives. For each event, they may choose Match, Partial, Miss, or Cannot judge. A claim about a feeling, general personality, or universal era event is not a concrete event hit. Do not persuade them to change a rating.

## Five short questions after reading

1. Which sentence felt most specific and useful? Why?
2. Which sentence could fit almost anyone, or felt wrong?
3. Did any year suggest the same story as another year?
4. Was anything confusing, too technical, or uncomfortable?
5. What question did you arrive hoping the site would answer but it did not?

Optionally ask for 1–5 ratings of clarity, distinctiveness between years, relevance, trust, and ease of navigation. Do not ask “Was it accurate overall?” as the only measure.

## Session record for the project team

Record a random tester ID, broad age band, setting, known/unknown birth time, novice/experienced, language, pre-recorded timeline yes/no, number of rated claims, distribution of all four labels, five short answers, and whether they reached the next page. Retain the original claim wording only if the tester deliberately exports and shares it. Keep “Cannot judge” in the denominator and report it separately. Do not compute or market a single accuracy percentage from a small self-selected cohort.

Target at least two willing testers in each age band (18–24, 25–39, 40–59, 60+), across at least two cultural/geographic settings. This is a coverage target, not a claim of statistical representativeness. Pause promotion if the same privacy, sensitive-content, or technical failure repeats.
